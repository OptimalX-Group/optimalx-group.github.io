Assistant Professor Yue Yu's research focuses on developing efficient and scalable algorithms for controlling autonomous systems in aerospace engineering. His work centers on numerical optimization and control theory, extending to various domains such as game theory, machine learning, and network systems. Recent research projects include real-time trajectory optimization and game-theoretic coordination in multiagent systems, with an emphasis on applications in advanced air mobility and spacecraft control.
