### Research Interests

I'm working on optimizing the trajectories of vehicles to obtain the most informative sensor measurements possible. This work lies at the intersection of guidance (trajectory design), and navigation (state estimation from measurements), and has applications in search-and-rescue, environmental monitoring, and autonomous landing of spacecraft.
