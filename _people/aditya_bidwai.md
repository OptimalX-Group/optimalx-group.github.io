### Bio

<!-- Ansh recieved degrees in Physics and Psychology from University of California - Santa Barbara in 2023. He will start his PhD at UPenn in Fall 2024 in the Psychology Department supported by the NSF-GRFP. -->


I am a graduate student in Robotics at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, beginning in September 2024. Previously, from December 2022 to July 2024, I worked as a Research Engineer at the Multi-Agent Robotic Motion (MARMot) Laboratory in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the National University of Singapore (NUS), under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Guillaume Sartoretti. I completed my undergraduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from BITS Pilani, Goa Campus, India, in 2022.

### Research Interests

<!-- Ansh is interested in utilizing machine learning methods to better understand human perception and taking inspiration from the human brain to create improved machine learning algorithms. -->

I am broadly interested in robotics and autonomy, with a specific focus on the synergy between perception and planning. My goal is to enable robots to interact with their environment with enhanced awareness of their objectives and the state of the environment.
